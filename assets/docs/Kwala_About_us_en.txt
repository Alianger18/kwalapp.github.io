# About Kwala

## Your Stress-Free Meal Companion

Kwala was created to do one simple thing: **erase the crazy fees and hidden surprises** that overwhelm users and make ordering frustrating.  

No inflated prices, no fake free deliveries, no sneaky service charges — just the food you want, at the price it’s meant to be.

---

## Our Mission

We believe ordering food should be **easy, transparent, and joyful**.  

Kwala’s mission is to let you enjoy meals exactly as you see them, customize them effortlessly, and always know what you’re getting — with no surprises.  

We’re here to put users at ease and make meal ordering fun again.

---

## Our Story

We noticed a problem: most food delivery apps overwhelm users with hidden fees, misleading images, and complicated customization.  

People were paying more than they expected, unsure of what they’d actually get.

So, we built **Kwala**.

Here, every meal is priced as it is in the restaurant, delivery fees are fair and location-based, and pickup is always free if you prefer it.  

Every menu item comes with professional photos — no stock images, no guesswork.  

Customizing orders is interactive, easy, and even fun.

In short: **what you see is what you get.**

---

## What We Offer

With Kwala, you can:

- Enjoy easy and interactive meal customizations
- See exactly what you’re ordering with real photos
- Pay only fair, transparent delivery fees or choose free pickup
- Cancel orders freely within the allowed period

We’re committed to giving you full control over your meals while keeping the experience playful and delightful.

At Kwala, **trust comes first**. We promise:

- Ease: Quick, interactive customizations and ordering
- Joy: A fun, stress-free experience every time you order
